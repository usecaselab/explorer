## Problem

Revenue-based financing lets a business raise capital and repay investors a set percentage of revenue until a return cap, instead of taking on fixed debt or giving up equity. Repayment depends on the business reporting revenue honestly and routing the payments on schedule, so investors fund against numbers they cannot see directly. Enforcement comes down to an audit clause or a lawsuit, which prices small issuers and small investors out of the instrument.

## Solution

A revenue-share bond where incoming revenue passes through a contract that routes the agreed percentage to bondholders as it arrives and stops once the return cap is reached. Repayment is a property of the revenue flow rather than something the issuer has to remember to do or investors have to chase, and the outstanding balance is visible to both sides at every point.

## Why Ethereum

When the issuer controls the books and the bank account, investors in a revenue-share deal depend on self-reported revenue and the issuer's willingness to remit on time. Settling the split onchain makes the repayment rule and the full payment history something both sides verify directly, so a small business can raise this way without investors needing to fund an audit to check the accounting.
