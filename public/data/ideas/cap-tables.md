## Problem

Startup cap tables live in expensive, siloed platforms like Carta where ownership records can fall out of sync, transfers require manual legal work, and founders have no real-time, verifiable source of truth.

## Solution

A startup's cap table kept as an onchain record from incorporation onward, with issuance, vesting, and transfers written to it as they happen. Founders, investors, and employees read the same source of truth directly, and a transfer updates the record rather than requiring separate legal work to reconcile the official version later.

## Why Ethereum

When a cap table lives on a vendor's platform, founders and investors depend on that company to keep the record accurate, grant access, and not change its terms or pricing, and the official version can quietly diverge from what shareholders believe they hold. Recording ownership onchain gives every party the same signed record, verifiable directly and not held hostage to one provider.
