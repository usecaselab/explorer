## Problem

Small businesses and exporters wait 30-90 days for payment on legitimate invoices and receivables, while factoring intermediaries charge steep discount rates and lenders reject applications because documents are unverifiable PDFs prone to double-pledging.

## Solution

Tokenized invoices and receivables that can be verified, sold, collateralized, or traded in real time, so a business can raise cash against money it is owed without waiting out the payment term. A lender checks the same ownership record everyone else sees, which is what stops a single receivable from being pledged to several lenders at once.

## Why Ethereum

When invoices are PDFs and factoring runs through one intermediary, lenders depend on that intermediary's word and the same receivable can be pledged to several of them at once. Putting invoices onchain gives every lender the same ownership record to verify directly, so authenticity does not rest on a single platform and the same invoice cannot be sold twice.
