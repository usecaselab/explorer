## Problem

When a food safety recall is issued, tracing contaminated product back to the source takes days or weeks because lot IDs change at every handoff between grower, packer, distributor, and retailer — each maintaining their own disconnected records — meaning contaminated product stays on shelves while investigators manually reconstruct the chain.

## Solution

A common lot ID standard that persists immutably through every handoff in the supply chain — so when a retailer scans a product, they can trace it back to the specific farm field and harvest date in seconds, and a recall can be executed against a precise subset of product rather than pulling entire categories from shelves.

## Why Ethereum

A traceability platform run by one large retailer or distributor would let that operator decide who sees the chain and could quietly edit records that point back to its own suppliers. Recording each handoff onchain keeps the history outside any one company's control, so a recall can be traced and food fraud caught by anyone in the chain rather than only the host.
