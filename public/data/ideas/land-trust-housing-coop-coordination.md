## Problem

Land trusts and housing cooperatives are structured as 501(c)(3)s or limited equity co-ops, with governance rules buried in paper bylaws that members rarely read, share transfers processed through manual legal work, and financial reporting that requires expensive annual audits. The overhead scales linearly with the number of units and makes it prohibitively expensive to operate community-owned housing at anything beyond neighborhood scale.

## Solution

A governance and ownership coordination layer that sits on top of existing legal entities. Membership transfers automate within the constraints of the ground lease or co-op agreement, governance votes and financial reporting are verifiable to members in real time, and the per-unit administrative overhead drops to a level that lets CLT and co-op structures scale.

## Why Ethereum

When a land trust or co-op runs on paper bylaws and outside administrators, members rarely see how governance and money actually move, and they depend on staff or a vendor to process transfers and report honestly. Putting the coordination layer onchain lets members verify votes and distributions directly, keeping control of the organization with the people who belong to it.
