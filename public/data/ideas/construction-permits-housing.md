## Problem

A residential construction project, whether it is adding units, building an accessory dwelling, or redeveloping a lot, passes through a sequence of agency signoffs (planning, zoning, building, fire, environmental, sometimes utilities) where each step can take weeks or months and the applicant has no real-time view of which desk the file currently sits on. The delays and discretion at each stage feed the housing-supply gap that drives affordability problems in many cities, and applicants without political access tend to wait longer or get steered through more requirements than connected developers.

## Solution

A permit case file recorded onchain from submission through final sign-off, with each agency review, inspection result, and decision rationale logged as it happens. The applicant sees where the permit sits in real time, the public can compare which projects move quickly and which stall, and deviations from the standard timeline or procedure surface against a shared baseline rather than hiding inside one office's filing system.

## Why Ethereum

When permit timelines and decisions live inside agency systems, the same officials whose discretion the public is meant to check also control the record of how that discretion was exercised, and an applicant or a journalist has no independent way to see where in the chain a project is stuck or why. Anchoring the case file onchain keeps the timeline and the signoffs auditable by anyone, so a city's commitment to predictable permitting becomes visible rather than something residents have to take on trust.
