## Problem

Scientific knowledge is scattered across millions of papers and databases with no common layer connecting a finding to the data behind it, the studies that replicated or failed to replicate it, or the retractions that revised it. Whether a given result still stands is hard to see, and cross-domain connections are found mostly by accident.

## Solution

An onchain knowledge graph that links papers to their underlying data, their replications, and their retractions, maintained as a public resource rather than inside one publisher's database. A researcher or an application can trace a finding's current standing and follow connections across disciplines without negotiating access.

## Why Ethereum

A knowledge graph owned by one publisher or database vendor would let that operator decide which findings connect, who can build on the layer, and what gets charged for access, recreating the silos the project is trying to dissolve. Building it onchain keeps the connective layer outside any single institution's control, so any researcher or application can link findings across disciplines without negotiating for permission.
