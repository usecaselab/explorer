## Problem

Small-scale renewable energy projects struggle to attract financing because ownership, cash flows, and performance data are not standardized or auditable, making it prohibitively hard to bundle many sites into investable instruments.

## Solution

Onchain funding models for solar, storage, and community energy projects that track ownership, cash flows, and performance

## Why Ethereum

When a financing platform holds the ownership records and reports the performance data, investors depend on that operator's books and it can favor its own deals or fold and strand the projects. Recording ownership, cash flows, and generation data onchain keeps them auditable by anyone, so small projects can be bundled and financed without trusting a single intermediary's accounting.
