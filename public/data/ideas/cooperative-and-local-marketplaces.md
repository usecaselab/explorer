## Problem

Small producers selling through conventional distribution channels lose most of their margin to intermediaries, while consumers who want to buy local have no transparent, community-governed platform to connect with them directly.

## Solution

Community-owned platforms that let producers sell directly and keep more economic value within local food systems

## Why Ethereum

A conventional marketplace operator sets the fees, owns the reviews and customer relationships, and can suspend a producer or change the rules, leaving small sellers dependent on a platform whose interests differ from theirs. A neutral marketplace layer no single company controls lets producers participate without lock-in and keep their reputation and customer ties as something they own.
