## Problem

One disputed complaint or an algorithm's mistake can deactivate a driver's or courier's account overnight, cutting off their income with no hearing and an appeals form that disappears into a queue. The work record the decision rests on, the trips completed, the ratings earned, the incident flagged, lives entirely in the platform's database, so the worker has no copy of the evidence and no standing to challenge how it was read. The platform is the record-keeper, the prosecutor, and the only court at once. A worker who is deactivated does not just lose this job; they lose the years of history that made them hireable, and they start over from zero on the next app with nothing to show for it.

## Solution

A worker-held account and work record where the platform writes each completed job, rating, and any deactivation together with its stated reason to a log the worker co-controls and an agreed neutral party can read. When an account is suspended, the reason and the signals behind it are recorded at the moment of the decision, so the worker can bring the case to a neutral forum, a workers' cooperative panel or an arbitration process, that reviews the same record the platform acted on rather than a closed support queue. A workable starting point is one platform or cooperative agreeing to write deactivation events and their reasons to a record the worker holds alongside it, with a simple appeal a panel decides against that record. Portable work history across platforms can follow.

## Why Ethereum

What a chain cannot do is force a hostile platform to record an honest reason for cutting someone off; the platform writes that line, and one willing to cooperate could simply hand the worker a signed export. What genuinely needs neutral ground is the work record itself: the years of completed trips, ratings, and finished jobs that make a worker hireable. Held as a record the worker carries across platforms rather than a row in one company's database, that history cannot be erased or withheld when an account is closed, so a deactivated worker reaches the next platform, or a cooperative panel, still holding proof of everything they built instead of starting from zero.
