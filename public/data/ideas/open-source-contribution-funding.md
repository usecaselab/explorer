## Problem

Open-source contributors build critical infrastructure but have no portable record of their contributions, and funding decisions are made by a small number of gatekeepers disconnected from the people doing the work.

## Solution

A portable record of who contributed what to open-source projects, with funding that flows against that record rather than through a handful of gatekeepers. Contributors carry their history across projects and platforms, and any funder can direct money toward the work using the same record, with reward flows that are visible to everyone.

## Why Ethereum

When contribution history and funding decisions sit inside one platform or with a few gatekeepers, that party decides whose work counts and can deplatform a contributor or steer money toward favored projects. Keeping contribution records and governance onchain lets the people doing the work carry their history anywhere, and any funder can recognize it without a central authority's approval.
