## Problem

Research funding is allocated by small expert panels reviewing grant applications, with no mechanism to aggregate what the wider community actually believes about which hypotheses will replicate, which drugs will fail in trials, or which directions will pay off. Researchers privately hold strong views, the lab next door quietly failed to reproduce a celebrated result, but those views rarely surface until a formal replication study lands years later, if one ever does. The cost of being wrong in public keeps people quiet, so the field's real confidence stays hidden. Funding then skews toward incremental work from established labs, because panels reward what looks safe rather than what the community privately rates as likely to hold.

## Solution

Markets where researchers stake on the outcome of specific, falsifiable claims, will this finding replicate, will this trial hit its primary endpoint, with the position settling against a pre-registered test and payouts flowing to those who were right. The market price becomes a live read on collective confidence, available well before a formal replication exists, and a researcher's record of correct calls becomes a credibility signal funders can weigh.

A workable starting point is a single high-stakes question set, such as the replication of a batch of widely cited results in one subfield, with claims resolved by a pre-agreed replication protocol. Keeping the scope to claims with a clear, scheduled resolution avoids the hardest disputes while the mechanism proves itself. Broader coverage and funding integrations come later.

## Why Ethereum

A prediction market run by one company depends on that operator to hold the stakes, settle honestly, and not exclude participants or views it finds inconvenient, and it can shut the market down when a price embarrasses a powerful funder. Running it onchain keeps the stakes, the resolution rules, and the track record outside any single operator's control and auditable by anyone weighing the signal, which matters most precisely when the market is contradicting people with the power to suppress it.
