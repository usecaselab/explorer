## Problem

A letter of credit — the bank guarantee that an importer will pay an exporter on delivery — still requires physical paper documents to be couriered between banks across countries, a process that takes 5–10 days and costs $500–1500 per transaction for what is fundamentally a message saying 'we guarantee payment if these conditions are met.'

## Solution

Smart contract-based letters of credit that issue, transfer, and settle digitally — releasing payment to the exporter automatically when shipping documents and delivery conditions are cryptographically verified, without physical document exchange.

## Why Ethereum

A digital letter-of-credit network owned by one bank or consortium would let those operators decide who can issue and clear, and route every trade through infrastructure they control and price. Running letters of credit onchain keeps the guarantee and its conditions outside any single institution's hands, so any bank can participate and counterparties can verify the terms themselves rather than trusting a courier and an intermediary.
