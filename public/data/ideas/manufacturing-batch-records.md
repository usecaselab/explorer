## Problem

Pharmaceutical and medical device manufacturers need batch records that regulators and partners can trust, but most store them in mutable databases where a quality engineer can change a test result after the fact without anyone noticing. Reconciling records across suppliers for a single product lot takes weeks of manual document retrieval, which slows recalls when speed matters most.

## Solution

Batch records that capture every production step, quality test, and deviation with cryptographic attestation at the moment of recording. The record cannot be revised afterward, and lot-level history can be traced across the whole supplier chain instantly when a recall is triggered.

## Why Ethereum

When batch records sit in a manufacturer's own mutable database, the company being audited controls the evidence and a quality result can be changed after the fact without anyone noticing. Committing each production step onchain puts the record beyond the manufacturer's reach to revise, so regulators and partners get an audit trail that holds because of how it is built rather than because a procedure was followed.
