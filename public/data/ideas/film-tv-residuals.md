## Problem

When a film or show is reused, streamed in a new window, licensed to another territory, or rerun, the actors, writers, and directors are owed residuals. Those payments are calculated and disbursed by studios and distributors from their own records of where and how often a title ran. Performers cannot see the underlying usage data, residual statements arrive long after the exhibition they cover, and underpayment is common enough that the guilds run standing audit programs to recover it.

## Solution

Residual terms encoded against verified distribution and streaming data, so that when a title runs in a new window the payment owed to each rights holder is computed and released from the same usage record everyone can see. A performer gets a residual that follows the actual exhibition of their work rather than a statement they have to take on trust.

## Why Ethereum

The studio or distributor that owes residuals is also the only party that records where and how often a title ran, so performers audit blind and chase payments well after the fact. Settling residuals onchain against verified distribution data puts the usage record and the payment rule where the guild and the performer can verify them directly, so a residual does not depend on the payer's own accounting.
