## Problem

Cross-border payroll and contractor payments route through multiple intermediaries, taking days to settle with high fees, while tax reporting and compliance for global teams remain a manual reconciliation nightmare.

## Solution

Payroll and contractor payments settled directly to workers onchain instead of routed through a chain of correspondent banks. The employer keeps control of the schedule and the funds, payouts arrive in minutes rather than days, and tax and KYC reporting is generated from the payment record instead of reconciled by hand across jurisdictions.

## Why Ethereum

Cross-border payroll routed through correspondent banks means each intermediary can delay, take a cut, or block a payment to a worker in a sanctioned-adjacent region, and the employer cannot see where money stalls. Running payouts onchain keeps the schedule and the funds under the employer's control and settles directly to workers without a chain of banks deciding whether the payment goes through.
