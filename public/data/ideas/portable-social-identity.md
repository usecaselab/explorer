## Problem

A creator who spends years building an audience on one platform holds none of the connection to it: the follower list, the messaging reach, and the engagement history all live in the company's database. When the account is suspended, the niche is ruled off-brand, or the app changes its rules or shuts down, the audience does not come along and the creator starts from zero somewhere else. The same lock-in hits a merchant whose reviews and buyer list stay behind on the marketplace they are trying to leave. And because follower counts and engagement run on metrics only the platform can see, they are easy to inflate and impossible for an outside party (a sponsor, a venue, another app) to verify independently.

## Solution

A handle, profile, and social graph the user holds in their own account, readable by any app that chooses to support it, so the link between a person and the people who follow them is not the property of one platform. Layered on top are signed attestations for engagement and participation (posts, contributions, verified interactions) that travel with the user and can be checked by an outside party rather than taken on the platform's word. A workable starting point is the portable follower graph alone, adopted by a cluster of apps in one niche that already compete for the same creators, so a creator who leaves one can rebuild their audience in another the same day. Richer reputation and cross-app engagement records can layer on once the graph itself is portable.

## Why Ethereum

On a conventional social platform the company owns the follower graph, so it can de-platform a creator or change the rules and the audience does not come along. Holding the handle and social graph onchain keeps the connection between a person and their audience with the people involved, readable by any app, and survivable if one platform turns hostile or disappears.
