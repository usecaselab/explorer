## Problem

Reporting that was published in good faith routinely vanishes from the record once the outlet that hosted it changes hands. When a publication is acquired, the new owner can quietly delete inconvenient stories, and the only trace left is a dead link. When a complaint is settled, taking a piece down is often part of the deal, and the correction never reaches the people who read the original. When an outlet simply folds, its archive goes offline with the renewal it stopped paying. In every case the journalist who did the work has no standing to keep their own published record intact, and readers and researchers are left citing pages that no longer exist.

## Solution

At the moment of publication, commit the finished piece to permanent storage and write an immutable, timestamped record of its content and the date it ran, keyed to the author. Because the stored copy cannot be overwritten, a later edit produces a new version alongside the original rather than replacing it, and the timestamped history makes any subsequent deletion or quiet rewrite visible against what was first published. The version of record can be cited and checked without the publisher's cooperation or continued existence. A workable starting point is an end-of-pipeline step a newsroom or a freelancer adds at publication: it stores the final article and its assets, writes a signed timestamp of the content onchain, and returns a permanent citation link, leaving the live site untouched while guaranteeing the published version cannot be unpublished after the fact.

## Why Ethereum

The threat here is not a complaint arriving today but a deletion, a quiet edit, or an expired domain arriving years later, after the outlet has been sold, settled, or folded. An archive the publisher controls is exactly the archive its next owner can rewrite. What matters is that the version of record outlives the company: an immutable, timestamped commitment to what was published and when means a later takedown or silent revision shows up against the original instead of replacing it, and the work stays citable even after the outlet that ran it no longer exists to vouch for it.
