## Problem

Intellectual property rights are indivisible and illiquid, making it nearly impossible for small investors to participate in IP financing, for researchers to crowdfund early-stage IP, or for inventors to unlock the dormant value of their portfolios.

## Solution

Fractional ownership, crowdfunding, financing, and trading of intellectual property rights through IP-NFTs and tokenized IP instruments

## Why Ethereum

A platform that hosts fractional IP shares controls who can buy in, what trades clear, and which projects get listed, and it can delist an asset or freeze a market to protect its own interests. Issuing these rights onchain keeps ownership records and transfers outside any one operator's reach, so inventors and small investors hold positions that stay tradable without asking permission.
