## Problem

Digital media is trivially fakeable. A photo, video, or document circulating online has no widespread way to be checked against the source it claims, and the same authoring tools that produce convincing media also produce convincing fakes. Newsrooms whose footage is doctored, creators whose work is plagiarized or attributed to someone else, and viewers trying to figure out what is real all face the same problem with no shared answer.

## Solution

Media signed at the point of capture and through each subsequent edit, building an authenticity chain anyone can check. A photo, video, or document carries a verifiable trail back to the device or person that produced it, edits register against that record, and a fabrication shows up against the original signed content rather than competing with it on visual plausibility alone.

## Why Ethereum

An authenticity record only works if people who distrust each other can check it independently, and a registry run by one platform or government can be pressured to revoke signatures or favor certain sources. Recording the authenticity chain onchain keeps the history of who signed what verifiable by anyone, without the hosting platform having to cooperate or stay neutral.
