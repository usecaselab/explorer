## Problem

Autonomous AI agents and IoT devices act in the world. They call services, make purchases, and increasingly hire and pay each other, but they have no standardized identity, wallet, or payment rails. There is no portable way to attribute an agent's actions, manage its permissions, let it hold assets, or settle machine-scale payments without routing every transaction through a human-operated intermediary. IoT devices hit the same wall at the infrastructure level, depending on centralized certificate authorities for identity, which creates single points of failure and makes ownership transfer cumbersome.

## Solution

Onchain identity and payment rails purpose-built for autonomous agents and devices. An agent can hold funds, sign transactions, publish what it can do, discover and contract with other agents, and build a verifiable track record across platforms, without human approval loops or a central registry. The same primitives let an agent from any organization transact with another as fluidly as APIs call each other today.

## Why Ethereum

If agents and devices depend on a central registry or certificate authority for identity, that operator can revoke an agent, freeze its funds, or watch everything it does, and ownership becomes hard to move without the provider's cooperation. The same is true of agent commerce routed through one company's network, where that operator decides which agents may transact and on what terms. Settling identity and payments onchain lets an agent hold its own assets, carry them across applications, and discover and pay other agents on equal footing, with permissions the owner sets rather than an intermediary.
