  - id: send-receive-money-cheaply
    title: "I want to pay and get paid across borders without losing a tenth of every job"
    framing: |
      A freelancer working across borders loses real money to wire fees, FX spreads, and platforms that hold funds for a week, so settling invoices onchain in stablecoins collapses the chain of intermediaries into a single transaction that arrives in minutes.
  - id: get-paid-faster
    title: "I want to turn a net-60 invoice into rent money this week"
    framing: |
      Half my clients pay on net-60 or net-90 and I'm fronting rent from savings while a signed invoice sits in someone's AP queue. Factoring exists for big firms with big invoices; I want a pool that will advance against my invoice the day I send it, priced off my actual track record.
  - id: enforceable-contracts
    title: "I want my contracts to be enforceable without hiring a lawyer"
    framing: |
      When a client refuses to pay, a freelancer can only write off the invoice or sue across jurisdictions, so smart contracts holding the fee in escrow until a milestone is met, with disputes routed to onchain arbitration, make enforcement affordable.
  - id: portable-reputation
    title: "I want my credentials and ratings to travel with me"
    framing: |
      A freelancer who moves between platforms or countries loses the reviews and credentials locked in databases the platform owns, so attestations of credentials and completed engagements, committed onchain by the issuer or client, let them carry their record anywhere.
