    title: "I want my medical records under my own control"
    framing: |
      A patient rebuilds their history from memory at every appointment because each provider's records sit in a separate system, so health records in patient-controlled storage with onchain access permissions let them grant and revoke provider access in seconds.

  - id: get-paid-faster
    title: "I want insurance payouts when the trigger is met, not when an adjuster decides"
    framing: |
      Patients pay premiums for years and then wait weeks while an adjuster decides whether an admission qualified, so parametric insurance contracts settled onchain against verifiable triggers like admission records and diagnostic codes pay out the moment the condition is met.
    ideas: [parametric-health-insurance]

    title: "I want to see and challenge the algorithm that decided my case"
    framing: |
      Denials and prior authorization decisions run through models the insurer will not disclose, so adjudication models with weights and decision logs committed onchain let a patient see which model judged them and challenge it through neutral arbitration.
    ideas: [ai-adjudicators, insurance-claims-adjudication]
  - id: verify-what-im-buying
    title: "I want to verify the drugs and treatments I am taking are real"
    framing: |
      Counterfeit medications kill hundreds of thousands a year and a patient cannot tell a real pack from a fake, so onchain provenance with each supply-chain handoff signed lets a pharmacist scan a pack and verify a legitimate manufacturer in seconds.
    ideas: [pharmaceutical-traceability]
  - id: trust-the-evidence
    title: "I want to trust the trial results behind the drugs I am prescribed"
    framing: |
      Trial sponsors hold the only copy of source data and can revise analyses or bury bad outcomes unnoticed, so pre-registered hypotheses and source data committed onchain at capture give doctors a record that cannot favor the sponsor's conclusion.
    ideas: [decentralized-clinical-trials, pre-registration-and-results]
