    title: "I want to see where my tax money actually went"
    framing: |
      Public budgets are executed in spreadsheets nobody sees and summarized in reports a year out of date, so government payments executed onchain, with each transfer traceable from appropriation to vendor, let anyone follow spending in real time.
  - id: verify-public-decisions
    title: "I want to know why my permit was denied and who decided"
    framing: |
      When a zoning appeal, a benefits claim, or a contract award goes against me, I get a one-line outcome and no record of who weighed what. A tamper-evident log of which official, which rule, and which inputs produced the decision would let me actually contest it.
  - id: own-my-records
    title: "I want my civil records under my own control, not stuck in the office that issued them"
    framing: |
      Birth, marriage, and death records govern every official interaction yet sit in physical files that can be lost or held hostage to a fee, so civil registries that issue them as signed onchain attestations let a citizen hold their proof.
  - id: enforce-the-rules-already-on-the-books
    title: "I want environmental and housing rules enforced when they're broken, not years later"
    framing: |
      Environmental thresholds and housing covenants are rules whose enforcement lags so far behind violations that the harm is done first, so continuous measurement piped to onchain rule engines records a violation the same hour a threshold is crossed.
