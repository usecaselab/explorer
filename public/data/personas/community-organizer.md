desires:
  - id: coordinate-with-neighbors
    title: "I want a local currency for my neighborhood that doesn't die when the operator quits"
    framing: |
      We've tried timebanks and local scrip and every one of them folded when the person running the spreadsheet moved on or the platform shut down. I want issuance rules, balances, and redemption to live somewhere the community keeps running even when I step back.
  - id: pool-capital-with-peers
    title: "I want to pool capital with my neighbors without paying a bank to do it"
    framing: |
      Lending circles and mutual-aid pools cost money to administer through banks and hold more cash than they need, so onchain lending pools set contribution and disbursement rules in code and return the savings to members instead of a custodian.
  - id: fund-our-own-infrastructure
    title: "I want my block to finance its own solar roof or fiber line and pay investors back from what it earns"
    framing: |
      Our co-op solar project is too small for a bank and too structured for a GoFundMe, so it just doesn't happen. I want a way to take small investments from neighbors and outside backers and pay them back from the meter revenue, with the repayment schedule something they can verify rather than trust me on.
  - id: audit-public-money
    title: "I want to watch the money my neighborhood voted on actually move"
    framing: |
      Participatory-budgeting votes disappear into a general-fund line item the community cannot track, so budget allocations executed through onchain payments, with each transfer traceable to the project it was meant to fund, close the gap between the vote and the spending.
  - id: enforce-the-rules-already-on-the-books
    title: "I want the affordable-housing and climate commitments my city signed to actually hold"
    framing: |
      Cities quietly fall out of compliance with their housing covenants and emissions targets because enforcement is starved and the data is unqueryable, so onchain registries fed by attested rent rolls and emissions data make non-compliance visible the moment it happens.
  - id: coordinate-collective-action
    title: "I want a rent strike or buyer's pledge that only triggers once enough neighbors are in"
    framing: |
      Every rent strike I've tried to organize dies in the "I'll do it if everyone else does" phase. I want people to be able to commit publicly with their pledge held in escrow, so the action goes live the moment we cross the threshold and nobody is exposed alone.
