  - id: coordinate-with-neighbors
    title: "I want my neighborhood to circulate money locally instead of leaking it to global platforms"
    framing: |
      Local-currency and cooperative-marketplace schemes collapse when the operator they run on raises fees or shuts down, so onchain community currencies keep the issuance schedule, redemption rules, and governance with the community rather than at the mercy of a vendor.
    ideas: [community-currencies, cooperative-and-local-marketplaces, peer-to-peer-energy-markets]
  - id: pool-capital-with-peers
    title: "I want to pool capital with my neighbors without paying a bank to do it"
    framing: |
      Lending circles and mutual-aid pools cost money to administer through banks and hold more cash than they need, so onchain lending pools set contribution and disbursement rules in code and return the savings to members instead of a custodian.
    ideas: [cooperative-lending-pools]
  - id: fund-our-own-infrastructure
    title: "I want my neighborhood to fund its own solar, broadband, and housing"
    framing: |
      Neighborhood-scale solar, broadband, and housing projects are too small for conventional bond underwriting and too large for crowdfunding, so onchain bond issuance with standardized performance reporting lets a neighborhood raise capital at a scale that matches its actual project.
    ideas: [local-infrastructure-bonds, renewable-project-financing, land-trust-housing-coop-coordination]
  - id: audit-public-money
    title: "I want to watch the money my neighborhood voted on actually move"
    framing: |
      Participatory-budgeting votes disappear into a general-fund line item the community cannot track, so budget allocations executed through onchain payments, with each transfer traceable to the project it was meant to fund, close the gap between the vote and the spending.
    ideas: [participatory-budgeting, community-space-allocation]
  - id: enforce-the-rules-already-on-the-books
    title: "I want the affordable-housing and climate commitments my city signed to actually hold"
    framing: |
      Cities quietly fall out of compliance with their housing covenants and emissions targets because enforcement is starved and the data is unqueryable, so onchain registries fed by attested rent rolls and emissions data make non-compliance visible the moment it happens.
    ideas: [affordable-housing-covenants, city-climate-commitment-tracking]
  - id: coordinate-collective-action
    title: "I want a way to get enough people to commit at once for a coordinated action to actually start"
    framing: |
      Coordinated action like a strike or a buyer's pledge stalls because no one will move first, so onchain commitment markets let participants stake a pledge that releases only once a threshold of others have signed on.
    ideas: [hyperstition-markets]
