desires:
  - id: get-paid-faster
    title: "I want to be paid the moment the work is done"
    framing: |
      A driver or contractor often waits a week or more for pay because the platform reconciles on its own cycle, but smart contracts that release funds the moment a delivery is signed for, against signals already collected, close the gap to minutes.
  - id: portable-reputation
    title: "I want my ratings and history to travel with me"
    framing: |
      A worker's ratings and job history are an asset built over thousands of hours yet trapped in a platform's database, so signed onchain attestations of completed jobs and ratings, issued by the platform but owned by the worker, let that reputation travel anywhere.
  - id: access-to-credit
    title: "I want lenders to see the income I actually earn"
    framing: |
      Banks underwrite against W-2s and tax returns, so a gig worker with steady income across three platforms reads as irregular deposits and gets declined, but verifiable onchain payment histories from the platforms they use give a lender something concrete to underwrite.
  - id: coverage-for-a-single-shift
    title: "I want insurance I can switch on for one shift, not a yearly policy built for a salary"
    framing: |
      Traditional policies assume a steady job and an annual premium, so the cover that would actually protect me, against an injury on a delivery run, damage to the car I rent by the hour, or a cancelled gig, either does not exist for someone with my income pattern or costs more per year than I clear in a month. Coverage I can turn on for the hours I am working and pay for by the shift, settling as fast as the work itself pays, would fit how I actually earn.
  - id: due-process-when-deactivated
    title: "I want a way to contest being kicked off the app, not a form that vanishes"
    framing: |
      One disputed complaint or an algorithm's mistake can deactivate my account overnight, cutting off my income with no hearing and an appeals form that disappears into a queue. An account and work record I hold myself, with deactivations and their stated reasons logged where a neutral party can review them, gives me a way to actually contest a suspension instead of starting over from zero on another platform.
