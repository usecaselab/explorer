desires:
  - id: get-paid-faster
    title: "I want subsidy and insurance payments to arrive when the loss happens, not months later"
    framing: |
      Agricultural subsidies and weather insurance routinely arrive months after the gap they exist to cover has swallowed the season, so smart contracts triggered by satellite rainfall data or government attestations release funds onchain the day a threshold is crossed.
  - id: access-to-credit
    title: "I want to borrow against my land and my standing crop without paying 80 percent interest"
    framing: |
      Smallholders are invisible to banks because pre-harvest credit has no collateral and their land carries no recognized title, so tokenized future-crop receipts and onchain land titles give a lender collateral it can price and repaid automatically at harvest sale.
  - id: paid-for-value-i-drive
    title: "I want to get paid for the cover crops and hedgerows I plant, not just the bushels I sell"
    framing: |
      Buyers price my maize by weight and ignore the soil work that took years, while carbon programs gate payouts behind audits that cost more than a smallholder will ever earn from them. Satellite measurement plus onchain payouts would let me earn for the carbon and habitat my practices build without an auditor pocketing the margin.
  - id: verified-origin-without-the-fee
    title: "I want premium buyers to verify our quality without $10,000 in certification fees"
    framing: |
      Specialty buyers pay more for verified origin, but paper certification costs fall hardest on the small producers most likely to meet the standard, so onchain provenance for each lot lets a cooperative document quality cheaply and keep the premium.
  - id: share-a-finite-resource-honestly
    title: "I want water use measured so the neighbors with deeper wells stop draining the aquifer"
    framing: |
      In drought-prone regions overuse is profitable and undetectable under self-reported metering until the aquifer is gone, so onchain water-rights markets with continuous metering make overuse visible per well and let a farmer sell unused rights to a neighbor.
