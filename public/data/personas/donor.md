    title: "I want to see where my money actually went"
    framing: |
      Donations are pooled and regranted through accounting the donor never sees, severing the link between a gift and a household, so settling transfers onchain with every hop visible lets a donor follow a contribution from gift to recipient.

  - id: pay-for-outcomes
    title: "I want to pay for delivered outcomes, not for promises"
    framing: |
      Grants are paid upfront against proposed outcomes and reported on by the same organization seeking the next grant, so conditioning payments on independently verified milestones attested onchain aligns funding with what programs achieve rather than how they tell the story.
    ideas: [impact-certificates, outcome-based-funding, milestone-scholarship-disbursement]
  - id: participatory-grantmaking
    title: "I want the people a program serves to have a say in how the money is spent"
    framing: |
      Grant budgets are set by program officers working at a distance from the households a program serves, so the people with the most direct knowledge of what is needed have no hand in the allocation, but routing those budgets through onchain participatory allocation, where recipients vote on how funds are split and every transfer is traceable to the decision behind it, lets a donor fund choices made by the people closest to the need.
    ideas: [participatory-budgeting]
